Find your people when the network fails.

20.07 keeps a group connected with no signal at all: phones relay for each other over Bluetooth, and an optional internet bridge carries your group further, even with Bluetooth off. Free, open source, no accounts.

What you get

Live radar

See which way, and how far, your group is. Works on Bluetooth alone, with a hot/cold fallback if you decline location.

SOS that goes first

A flagged SOS jumps the queue on every path: Bluetooth and internet. Quiet messages and a loud alert are separate on purpose.

Last seen

A mate who dropped out shows as "Priya, 14 min ago, 3.2 km NE" for up to six hours. Held in memory only.

Photos and files

Share incident photos and files up to 400 KB. They hop over Bluetooth and, if you allow it, over the internet.

Internet reach (optional)

One switch. A phone with Wi-Fi or mobile data carries your group's encrypted traffic to mates out of Bluetooth range. Off by default.

Stranger carrying

Phones outside your group, with the switch on, can carry your sealed frames onward. They see sizes and timing, never content.

How it works

Make a group. One code or QR creates a random key. Share it in person; the app never brokers trust.
Phones relay for each other. Bluetooth links form a mesh; positions, messages, SOS and photos hop between members.
Out of range? The bridge. With Internet reach on, any phone that has a connection sends and receives for the group through public relays, so two phones with Bluetooth off can still talk.

Safety and privacy, plainly

  • Everything is sealed with your group's key before it leaves the phone. Relays and carriers can't read it.
  • Relays do see your phone's internet address, message sizes and timing, and a short rotating group code. Some relays keep what they receive: positions up to 6 hours, messages up to 7 days.
  • Built to avoid clogging servers in a crowd: SOS first, files last, slow-downs under congestion, per-hour limits. Tested in simulation up to 10,000 users.
  • No account, no phone number, no servers of ours; nothing is sent to us. A seized phone holds no location history.

Honest status: pre-1.0 (0.10.1-dev). The internet bridge works on two phones with Bluetooth off and in simulation; three-phone and large-crowd field tests are still to do. Don't rely on it as your only safety device. Known limitations.

Install

There is no Play Store build: download the signed APK from GitHub Releases, open it, and allow installs from that source once. Share the same file with friends, offline, by any means. Details and building from source.
